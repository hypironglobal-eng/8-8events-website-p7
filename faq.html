Frequently Asked Questions

How does 8.8 Events verify vendors? ▼
Vendors must provide valid identification, business registration (where applicable), and portfolio evidence. Our admin team manually reviews and approves all vendor applications before they go live on the marketplace.
Is my payment secure? ▼
Yes. Payments are processed through licensed payment partners (e.g., Paystack). We ensure there is a clear booking record, cancellation policy, and dispute resolution process tied to every transaction.
What happens if a vendor cancels? ▼
If a vendor cancels prior to service delivery and no replacement is accepted, you are entitled to a full refund according to our standardized platform refund policy.
Can I get help finding a vendor? ▼
Absolutely. Because we operate a hybrid model, our Admin support team can assist you with vendor matching and quote follow-ups if you prefer not to manage the search entirely on your own.
How do vendor payouts work? ▼
Vendors receive payouts based on the agreed milestone terms or after successful service delivery is confirmed, ensuring trust and accountability for both parties.
