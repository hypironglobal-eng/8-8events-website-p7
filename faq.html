For Vendors

Register using the Vendor Registration form, complete your business profile, upload verification documents, and our team will review and approve your account within 48 hours.

A percentage is deducted from each completed payout. The exact rate is communicated during onboarding. Premium plan vendors enjoy a reduced commission.

Payments are released to your registered bank account within 24–48 hours of a client confirming successful delivery. Payouts are processed through our licensed payment partners.
