The Process

How 8.8 Events Works

A simple, protected process for both event hosts and event professionals.

1

Create a Free Account

Sign up in minutes. Share your state and LGA so we can connect you with local professionals who understand your area.

2

Search & Request Quotes

Browse vendors by category, location, and price range. Send quote requests to multiple vendors and compare their offers.

3

Review & Confirm Booking

Review proposals, check verified reviews, and confirm your booking with clear written terms — no verbal agreements.

4

Pay Securely & Celebrate

Your payment is held securely. Vendors are paid only upon successful event delivery. Dispute support is available if anything goes wrong.

Register & Get Verified

Create your vendor account, upload your portfolio, and complete our verification process to earn a Verified badge.

2

List Your Services

Add your service packages with clear pricing and descriptions. Specify which states and LGAs you serve.

3

Receive & Respond to Requests

Get notified of client quote requests. Respond with your best offer and negotiate terms through the platform.

4

Deliver & Get Paid

Deliver your service, mark it complete on the platform, and receive your payout securely via our payment partner.
