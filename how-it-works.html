How 8.8 Events Works

8.8 Events is a hybrid marketplace that brings trust and structure to event planning in Nigeria. Whether you are a first-time host or an experienced planner, we make it easy to find and book reliable vendors.

For Customers (Event Hosts)

Planning an event shouldn't be stressful. We've simplified the process of finding and paying vendors.

  • Discover & Compare: Browse our directory of verified MCs, DJs, caterers, and planners across our launch states (Abuja, Kano, Lagos).
  • Request Quotes: Found a vendor you like? Send them your event details, date, and budget to get a custom quote.
  • Book Securely: Pay through our licensed payment partners. Your booking is protected, and cancellation terms are clear from the start.
  • Get Support: If you need help matching with a vendor, our Admin team is here to assist you through the hybrid support model.

For Vendors

Join a marketplace that values professionalism and helps you grow your event business.

  • Create a Profile: Set up your business profile, upload your portfolio, and list your service packages and pricing.
  • Get Verified: Pass our verification checks to build trust with customers instantly.
  • Receive Leads: Get quote requests directly from customers planning events in your area.
  • Guaranteed Payouts: Track your bookings and get paid reliably after delivering your services.
