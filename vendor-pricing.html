Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. You can create and maintain a verified vendor profile at no cost. We only earn a commission when you complete a booking through the platform.

What is the platform commission?

A small percentage is deducted from each completed booking payout. Premium plan vendors enjoy a reduced commission rate. Exact rates are shared upon registration.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your vendor dashboard.

How do payments work?

Client payments are held securely by our licensed payment partners and released to you after successful delivery of services.
