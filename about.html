Our mission is to eliminate the stress, fraud, and unreliability often associated with event planning. We empower customers to discover, compare, and book verified event vendors with confidence.

The Problem We Solve

Why 8.8 Events Exists

People planning events in Nigeria often struggle to find reliable vendors, confirm quality, and pay with confidence. Vendors struggle to build trust beyond social media. We fix this with a hybrid marketplace — structured booking records, verified vendors, and protected payments.
