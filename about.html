About 8.8 Events

We are building trust in the Nigerian event industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate the stress, fraud, and unreliability often associated with event planning. We empower customers to discover, compare, and book verified event vendors with confidence, while providing professional vendors a platform to grow their businesses securely.

The Problem We Solve

People planning events in Nigeria often struggle to find reliable vendors, compare prices, confirm quality, and pay with confidence. Vendors also struggle to build trust beyond social media. The result is a high-stress planning experience. We fix this by introducing a hybrid marketplace with structured booking records, verified vendors, and protected payments.

Why "Hybrid"?

We combine the convenience of self-service online booking with the reassurance of human support. Our Admin team monitors the marketplace, verifies vendors, assists with dispute handling, and supports customers who need help matching with the right professionals.
